FAQ

Common questions.

Is it safe to sign in with my SP Group account?

Your password is only used once during sign-in and is never stored. Home Assistant keeps only the renewable session tokens SP returns, and diagnostics downloads automatically redact your address, account number, premise ID and tokens. Deleting the account or integration removes them.

Do I need a smart meter or SP as my electricity retailer?

No to both. With a smart meter you get half-hourly usage (hourly bars in the Energy dashboard); without one, SP provides billed monthly totals. If you buy electricity from an Open Electricity Market retailer, usage still works because SP reads the meters — Energy dashboard costs are estimated at SP's regulated tariff while the Last bill sensor shows the exact billed amount.

Do I need any API keys?

No. Every default and opt-in public feed works without keys. A free data.gov.sg API key is optional if you want higher rate limits, and a free LTA DataMall key unlocks three optional sources (MRT/LRT crowd density, EV chargers and LTA mall carparks).

Can I add more than one SP account, or use prepaid (PPMS)?

Yes. Click Add SP account again for additional premises — each gets its own device and Energy statistics. Prepaid (PPMS) accounts automatically expose a Prepaid credit sensor with a low-balance automation recipe.

Is this an official app from SP Group, NEA, LTA or GovTech?

No. This is an independent, MIT-licensed community project, not affiliated with or endorsed by SP Group, NEA, LTA, PUB, MOM, MOE, MUIS, MAS or GovTech. Public feeds use official open data under the Singapore Open Data Licence; SP account data uses the unofficial APIs behind the SP app.
